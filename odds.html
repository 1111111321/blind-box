    Rules

    1. Twelve per case: each box opens once, and never repeats inside the same case.
    2. Refill: when all twelve are open you can refill the case; the collection keeps counting.
    3. Guarantee: ten draws without Epic or better, and the tenth is Epic, Legendary or Secret.
    4. Records: draws, collection and the guarantee counter live in localStorage on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
    5. Clearing: "Clear records" in the collection wipes everything, and cannot be undone.

    Notes

    The odds live in the RARITY table at the top of app.js. Change a number there and both the draw and this table follow.

    A small front-end project: no server, no account. Refreshing or switching browsers starts a fresh save.
