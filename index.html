How it works

  • Twelve per case: each box opens once, and never repeats inside the same case.
  • Kept for you: everything you draw is added to the collection; duplicates are counted.
  • Guarantee: ten draws without Epic or better, and the tenth is Epic, Legendary or Secret.
  • On this device: records live in the browser's localStorage; a new device or cleared data starts over.
